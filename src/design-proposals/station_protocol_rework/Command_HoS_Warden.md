## Background

Too many command roles have a tendancy to end up in a "Job+" position through their tools and lack of direct responsabilities. 
This style of gameplay has no positive effects and often degrades the RP of command members into doing the jobs of the subordinates better.
Examples of this are:

1. CMOs using their hypo and gloves to just be faster medics, taking the jobs of others because they can just be better instead of leading the team
2. RDs just generaly being useless in their department
3. QMs acting like a cargotech with extra coms
4. HoP literaly doing nothing with their department (See Role Restructuring for more on HD)
5. CE just being an engineer with better tools and armour
6. Captain being the all access greytider, or stepping in to take over other's jobs
7. The HoS fighting the threats directly instead of having overwatch and direction (HoS will be expanded on seperately with warden)

While this is not the case every round, it is something that has very real impacts on how the roles are played, along with the expectations of their underlings.

# HoS & Warden

The existing dynamic of HoS and Warden has turned the warden into the keeper of security, who often has more over-all information than the HoS, and has left them trapped in their stuffy office while the HoS playes Secoff+.

Over the course of many extended days of discussion on this in the workgroup, and months of discussion prior the the workgroup we have found this not be an acceptable outcome. 
The HoS is supposed to be the HEAD of Security, a command role who is not to step in just like any other command member. 
As such this section has been created to outline what we wish to push for in the HoS's position, how the second in command (warden) will change, and our proposed changes accordingly.

The position of the HoS should be within security, dealing with the command of troops, issuance of orders, ensurance of proper protocol adherance, and being the core of security's communications.
This means they will be spending the majority of their shift time within their department instead of preforming additional patrols, or engaging with the troops in common battles.

The position of the warden will be moved from simply having charge of prisoners and their needs, to instead being the second in command in true form, being able to mobilise as a boots on the ground/squad leader should the situation become dire accordingly.
